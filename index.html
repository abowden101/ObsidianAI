The platform

One control plane for analysis,
enforcement, and evidence.

Threat analysis

Describe suspicious activity in plain language. ObsidianAI correlates it against your environment and returns a prioritized assessment — not a raw alert feed.

Firewall rule generation

Turn policy intent into production-ready firewall rules in seconds. Review, approve, and deploy — with every change logged.

Compliance automation

Audit-ready reports and policy evidence generated from real activity — built for teams operating under SOC 2, HIPAA, or PCI-DSS obligations.

Natural-language policy

Write what you want enforced. ObsidianAI translates intent into segmentation, controls, and incident playbooks your team can audit.

How it works

From intent to enforcement
in three steps.

1

Connect

Point ObsidianAI at your firewall, logs, and asset inventory. Read-only to start — nothing changes without your approval.

2

Analyze

AI reasoning works your data continuously: flagging threats, finding gaps, and drafting the rules and reports to close them.

3

Act

You review and approve. Enforcement and evidence generation happen with a full audit trail behind every action.
